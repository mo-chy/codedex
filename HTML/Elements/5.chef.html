Chocolate Brownie 🍰🍫

Ingredients:

  • 🍫 72g cocoa powder
  • 🧈 135g unsalted butter<
  • 🥚21⁄2 eggs
  • 🍚 210g sugar
  • 🌾 36g flour
  • 🥜 150g nuts
  • 🌼 6ml vanilla extract
  • 🧂 teaspoon of salt

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 365°C.
  2. Grease or cover with sulfurized paper a square mold about 20 cm wide.
  3. Melt the butter with the coca powder, stirring on slow fire till get an homogenous mix.
  4. Remove from the fire and let cool for a few minutes.
  5. Pour into a medium sized container and add the eggs and vanilla.
  6. Beat it all with a whisk.
  7. In another container mix the flour, the sugar, the salt and the nuts.
  8. Incorporate into the main container and mix it.
  9. Fill the mold and bake for about 35-40 minutes.
  10. Wait for it to cool before cutting. Enjoy! 🤌
